How an invoice payment gets stolen

  • 1

    The email looks like your usual supplier. Same logo, same signature, a real invoice. Only the IBAN is different.

  • 2

    You pay. The bank executes the transfer and rarely gets it back.

  • 3

    You find out weeks later, when the supplier chases an invoice they think is unpaid.

Don't get robbed. Not by email, not by phone, not with an invoice.

Today's scams don't break your bank or your software: they convince a person to pay the wrong account. Verifix puts a confirmation in between, through a channel the fraudster does not control.

  • Main example

    Real invoice, changed IBAN

    The email looks like your usual supplier and the invoice is genuine. Only the account is different. Verifix spots it and holds the payment until someone confirms.

  • The boss's urgent email

    A message that looks like it comes from management asks for an urgent, confidential transfer. With Verifix, no new order leaves without confirmation outside email.

  • The cloned-voice call

    A voice can now be copied from a few seconds of audio. That is why a confirmation is worthless if it comes through the same channel: Verifix asks through another one, to the number or person you already know.

  • The payslip that changes account

    An email to HR asks to pay salary into a new account. Verifix treats that change like a supplier's: confirmation and a record.

Frequently asked questions

My bank already checks the payee. Why do I need this?

Your bank checks that the name you type matches the IBAN holder. If the fraudster gives you an account in their own name and you copy that name, or the warning is ignored, the payment goes out. Verifix knows which account that supplier used and stops the change.

Do you read my email?

No. We only read invoices, to extract the IBAN and the amount: those in your invoices inbox, those in your accounting software and, only if you turn on the Outlook or Gmail add-in, those that land in your own inbox.

Do I need to change bank or accounting software?

No. Verifix sits before the payment, with your usual bank and software. Odoo first; Holded, A3 and Sage next.
